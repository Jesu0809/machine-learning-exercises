Machine Learning: Concepts

Machine Learning is a branch of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn patterns and make decisions or predictions directly from data, instead of following explicitly programmed rules.

What is Machine Learning?

A field of artificial intelligence where systems learn patterns from examples instead of being explicitly told what to do for every possible case.

Main Purpose

To generalize from known examples so the system can make accurate predictions or decisions on new, previously unseen data.

Data → Training → Model → Prediction

📊
Data
Raw examples collected from the real world
⚙️
Training
The algorithm learns patterns from the data
🧠
Model
The trained result that captures those patterns
🎯
Prediction
The model's output for new, unseen input

Importance of Data

Data is the foundation of every model: the quality, quantity, and relevance of the training data directly determine how accurate and reliable the model's predictions will be. Poor or biased data leads to poor or biased predictions.

Simple Examples

  • Predicting a salary from years of experience
  • Filtering spam emails
  • Recommending movies or products
  • Recognizing objects in photos
