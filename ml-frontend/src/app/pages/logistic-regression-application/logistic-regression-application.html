Logistic Regression: Application

Classify a borrower as Low Risk or High Risk from their debt-to-income ratio.

1. Problem Context

A lender wants to flag which borrowers are likely to default before approving a loan. Each record describes one past loan: the borrower's debt-to-income ratio and whether the loan was repaid on time or ended in default. The model learns the relationship between that ratio and the outcome, and then classifies a new borrower into one of two classes — 0 = Low Risk (expected to repay) or 1 = High Risk (expected to default).
