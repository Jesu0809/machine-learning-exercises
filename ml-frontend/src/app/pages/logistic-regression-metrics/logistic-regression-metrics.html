F1-score
  • Accuracy — share of all test borrowers classified correctly.
  • Precision — of those flagged High Risk, how many really defaulted.
  • Recall — of the borrowers who really defaulted, how many the model caught.
  • F1-score — harmonic mean of precision and recall.

Interpretation. With accuracy around {{ m.accuracy | percent:'1.0-0' }} and precision and recall both close to it, the model performs well and in a balanced way: it does not achieve its accuracy by ignoring one of the two classes.
