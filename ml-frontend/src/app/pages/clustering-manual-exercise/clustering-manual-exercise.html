5. Final Result and Cluster Characteristics

By iteration 3 the assignments have mostly settled. Cluster 0 (Low risk) converges immediately after iteration 1 and never changes again: it groups applicants with a low debt-to-income ratio and high income, and its variance stays flat because that group is naturally well separated from the rest of the data.

Clusters 1 (Moderate risk) and 2 (High risk) keep exchanging a handful of borderline applicants between iterations 1 and 3: cluster 2's variance rises slightly as it absorbs points from the edge of cluster 1, while cluster 1's variance falls as it keeps its more central, consistent members. This is expected where two risk bands sit next to each other on a continuous scale rather than being cleanly separated.

The final grouping is interpretable: applicants with a low ratio and high income form a low-risk group, applicants with a high ratio and low income form a high-risk group, and everyone in between forms a moderate-risk group. This matches the same three risk profiles used later, with real data, on the Application page.
