Use Case 4: Autonomous Vehicle Navigation

Reinforcement Learning

Problem Context

Self-driving cars must make continuous, real-time decisions, such as when to accelerate, brake, or steer, in a constantly changing environment, in order to drive safely and efficiently without a human at the wheel.

Data That Could Be Used

Continuous streams of sensor data: camera images, LiDAR point clouds, radar, GPS position, and the outcomes of past driving decisions.

What the Model Predicts / Discovers

The optimal next action, such as steering angle, speed, or braking force, given the current state of the surrounding environment.

Type of Machine Learning

Reinforcement Learning: an agent learns by receiving rewards or penalties for its driving decisions, gradually improving its policy through experience.

Potential Benefit

Reduces accidents caused by human error and enables safer, more efficient transportation for everyone on the road.
