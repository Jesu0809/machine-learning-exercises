Linear Regression: Application

Estimate a monthly salary from years of work experience.

1. Problem Context

Companies need a quick, data-driven way to estimate a fair monthly salary for a candidate or employee based on their years of professional experience. This model analyzes the relationship between experience and compensation in a sample of the Colombian job market, so a recruiter or analyst can predict a reasonable salary value for a given number of years of experience, without manually inspecting the full dataset every time.

2. Independent Variable (X)

Years of Experience

Unit: years

Dependent Variable (Y)

Monthly Salary

Unit: Colombian Pesos (COP)

3. Dataset

  • Records: {{ totalRecords() }}
  • Independent variable: Years of Experience (years)
  • Dependent variable: Monthly Salary (COP)
  • Source: Synthetic sample dataset generated to represent the Colombian job market for this exercise, stored in LinearRegression.py.
