Use Case 1: Image Recognition for Medical Diagnosis

Supervised Learning

Problem Context

Radiologists must review large volumes of medical images, such as X-rays, MRIs, and CT scans, to detect anomalies like tumors. Manual review is time-consuming, requires years of training, and is subject to human fatigue, which can lead to missed diagnoses.

Data That Could Be Used

Thousands of medical scans labeled by specialists as "healthy" or "containing an anomaly", together with patient metadata such as age and scan type.

What the Model Predicts / Classifies

Whether a new scan shows signs of a tumor or abnormality, often highlighting the specific region of concern.

Type of Machine Learning

Supervised Learning (classification), commonly using Convolutional Neural Networks trained on labeled image datasets.

Potential Benefit

Faster and more consistent screening that helps radiologists prioritize urgent cases and catch anomalies earlier, improving patient outcomes.
