Use Case 2: Personalized Recommendation Systems

Supervised & Unsupervised Learning

Problem Context

Streaming platforms and online stores offer catalogs far too large for a user to browse manually. Without guidance, users struggle to find content or products they would enjoy, which hurts engagement and sales.

Data That Could Be Used

Historical user interactions such as views, purchases, and ratings, along with item metadata like genre, category, and price.

What the Model Predicts / Discovers

Which items a specific user is most likely to enjoy or purchase next, and which users share similar tastes.

Type of Machine Learning

A mix of Supervised Learning (predicting a rating or click) and Unsupervised Learning (clustering similar users or items via collaborative filtering).

Potential Benefit

Higher user engagement and sales by surfacing relevant content, which also improves customer retention over time.
