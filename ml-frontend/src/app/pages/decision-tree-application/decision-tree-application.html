Decision Tree: Application

Classify a credit card application as Approved or Denied from four applicant features.

1. Problem Context

A bank must decide whether to approve a credit card application. Each record describes one past applicant with four features — annual income, debt-to-income ratio, credit history length and number of open credit lines — and the decision that was made. The Decision Tree learns the rules behind those decisions and then classifies a new applicant into 0 = Denied or 1 = Approved.
